About Empower

We believe the people closest to a challenge are closest to its answer.

Since 2014, we have worked with communities to turn local knowledge into lasting progress.

Our approach

Listen first. Build together. Stay for the long term.

Empower is a community-led nonprofit working across learning, wellbeing, and opportunity. We bring funding, skills, and trusted relationships to ideas that are already growing locally.

Our role is to support, connect, and learn alongside the people who lead the work. Success means communities have more voice, more choice, and more resources to keep moving forward.

A community story

“The nearest water point was once hours away.”

Before the project began, Amina’s family spent nearly three hours each day fetching unsafe water from a distant stream. The water was muddy, difficult to store, and often caused illness in her young children.

Through Empower’s local partnership model, the community installed a protected water source and trained local technicians to maintain it. Families received practical guidance on water safety and long-term care, giving the project lasting value beyond the initial investment.

Today, Amina’s children reach a nearby tap in minutes, and her household has more time for school, work, and rest. The change is small in one moment, but life-changing over time.
